About

I have more than 20 years of academic experience at undergraduate and postgraduate levels, teaching Database Systems, Software Engineering, AI/ML and Data Analytics, and supervising many theses and capstone projects. I have taught at East West University, the Islamic University of Technology and Macquarie University.

My research focuses on data provenance, machine learning and big data analytics, with recent work on graph indexing, ML datasets and Bangla language processing. I hold a Ph.D. from the University of Twente and was a postdoctoral researcher at Macquarie University.

I am an expert in Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and have contributed to curriculum design, CQI processes and program outcome mapping aligned with BAETE and Washington Accord standards. I have served as an accreditation evaluator for BAETE since 2023 and have been involved in developing the M.Sc. and B.Sc. curricula in AI and Machine Learning at EWU.
